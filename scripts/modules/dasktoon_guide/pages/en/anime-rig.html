The Anime Rig

The anime rig gives a character a standard skeleton, swaying hair and skirts, and bend fixes at the knees, elbows, hips and shoulders. One command does it all; the panel lets you adjust it.

Rig in one go

Select the character's meshes (or select nothing to take every visible mesh) and choose Add › Armature › Anime Humanoid. DaskToon finds the body, fits the skeleton, makes the hair and skirt chains, paints the weights, makes the bend fixes and runs the Check (see The Rig Check). Without meshes selected the command adds just the standard skeleton.

The Anime Rig panel

Select the armature and open Properties › Object Data. The Anime Rig panel has the sub-panels Check, Joints, Parts, Regions, Gaze, Sway and Build. The Anime Rig panel in the Object Data of a mesh has two commands for Edit Mode: Add Part from Selection and Lock from Selection.

Joints

Edit Joints puts the skeleton in Edit Mode with X-Axis Mirror on, so both sides move together. Fit to Parts moves and scales the skeleton to the meshes of the parts; the joints you placed are replaced. Shoulder Follows Arm sets how much the shoulders lift when the arms rise (1 is a real shoulder, 0 keeps them still), and Bend Fixes turns the knee, elbow, hip and shoulder fix shape keys on or off at the next build.

Parts

Every mesh, or the faces of one material, or a vertex group, is a part with a Role: Body (skin and face), Clothing (follows the body), Accessory (rigid on one bone), Hair and Skirt (chains of bones), Jaw, Flat Eye and 2D Mouth (see Eyes, Mouth and Gaze). The buttons next to the list: + is Add Selected Meshes (the role comes from the name: hair, skirt, shirt…), the material icon is Add Material Part and the last is Remove Part. For Accessory, Hair and Skirt you can set the Bone the part hangs from; Bones per Chain says how many bones each lock or strip gets, and Chains how many strips go round a skirt. Highlight Part draws the chosen part in the 3D view.

Build

Build Rig makes the hair and skirt chains and paints the weights of every part. It replaces the weights of every part, so press it again after you change the parts or the joints.
