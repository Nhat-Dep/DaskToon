4. Hair

Hair moves on its own: DaskToon sways each lock with springs.

Separate parts

Keep the hair a mesh of its own (the clothes too), each lock a loose piece. DaskToon tells the body, the hair and the clothes apart by them, and sways each lock by itself. A character that is one solid piece gets ⚠. To split one: in Edit Mode select the hair, press P › Selection (or By Loose Parts).
