The Rig Check

The Check bends the character into test poses and lists what is wrong: joints outside the body, spots that tear, parts whose role looks wrong, a character that faces sideways.

Test poses

In the Check panel, the buttons Arms Up, Sit, Turn Head and Wave put the character in a test pose to see how the rig bends; nothing is keyed. Rest puts every bone back.

Check

Check runs the check; Check Again runs it again, and Done clears the list. It needs a standard skeleton. Each spot it finds is a box with a sentence and the button that fixes it. After a fix, press Build Rig to check again.

What it finds

A joint outside the body: Fix selects the joint in Edit Mode, move it into the body, then press Build Rig. A torn or stretched spot: Show puts the character in the pose that tears it and lights the spot up; Paint Regions lights it up and lets you paint the regions on its mesh. A part whose role looks wrong: Make it Hair or Make it a Skirt, or Make That Part a Skirt for the part of a mesh below the waist. A character that faces sideways: Turn 90° Left and Turn 90° Right turn it on the spot, then the rig is built and checked again.
