Hair and Skirt Sway

Hair and skirts made of chains of bones swing by themselves, with springs, gravity and colliders, without keying anything.

Live Sway

Build Rig makes the chains. In the Sway panel, Live Sway makes hair and skirts sway while the animation plays. Select a Hair or Skirt part to set how it sways: Stiffness (how strongly it springs back to its pose), Gravity, Drag (0 keeps swinging, 1 stops at once) and Radius (how far each joint keeps from the colliders).

Colliders

Hair and skirts do not go through the body. Build Rig makes capsules along the body bones as colliders; open Colliders under Sway to change the radius of each.

Baking

Bake Sway keys the sway on every frame of the scene, and Live Sway turns off so the keys play as they are. Use it before exporting animation that must play exactly the same elsewhere.

In the game

Sway in the Game (per part) says how the game engine does it: Runtime sways the chain while the game runs, with the spring bone of Unity or Godot; Baked plays the sway baked into the exported animation. If the bones of a part have sway keys and Runtime is on, it would sway twice, and the Engine Export warns you.
