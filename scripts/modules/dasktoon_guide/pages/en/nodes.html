Other Anime Nodes

Besides the Anime BSDF, DaskToon has nodes for materials, for parts of a character, and for single steps of the shading. Add them in the Shader Editor with Shift A › DaskToon Anime.

Materials

Anime Wood (hand-painted wood and bamboo), Anime Metal / Armor (high-contrast metal), Anime Fabric / Cloth (velvet sheen and silk color shift), Anime Tights & Fishnet (sheer tights and diamond fishnet), Anime Knit & Wool (stitch loops and fuzz), Anime Glass & Gem (sparkle and inner glow) and Anime Water & Liquid (stepped depth, foam and moving caustics).

Character features

Angel Ring draws the highlight ring on hair. Face Shadow smooths and directs the face shadow, from an SDF map or from the head direction; its Light Vector input follows the Sun of the scene. Anime Eye draws the iris, pupil, glow under the iris, the shadow of the upper lashes and sparkles. Warm/Cool Grade boosts saturation at the edge of the shadow and tints the lit side warm and the shadow cool. Anime Rim adds a crisp or soft rim light. Classic Cel is a plain cel shader node.

Single steps

Under Modular Pipeline, each part of the Anime BSDF is also a node of its own, to build your own order: Cel Shading, World Ambient, Light Tint, Crevice AO, Color Grade and Outline Ink. Prefer the Anime BSDF unless you need a different order. The Engine Export reads the Anime BSDF, Classic Cel, Anime Eye and Cel Shading nodes; the other nodes are shown in the game with the Anime BSDF and their colors.

Manga

The DaskToon Manga menu has Manga BSDF, Screentone (halftone dots, angle and sharpness), Cross-Hatching (pencil and ink hatching in levels) and Speed Lines (action and impact lines).
