Outlines

Outlines create anime line art around characters with a 3D inverted hull in the viewport and keep the lines when exported to games.

DaskToon Outlines

Open the material shader, and on the Anime BSDF node check Outline (or on the Cel Shading node choose Use Outline). DaskToon automatically creates a companion material named <material>.Outline and places the DaskToon Outline modifier at the end of the mesh stack. Lines thin down naturally on the lit side with a subtle hand-drawn pen wobble.

Outline Color

Adjust line thickness and color directly on the Anime BSDF node using Outline Width and Outline Color. The Outline Lighting Mix socket blends lines into the ambient scene light. Under Outline Mode, you can select Auto Harmonic (Kyoto Style) to synthesize deep tones from base colors, or Light Reactive.

Colored Lines for All Materials

Press Colored Lines for All Materials in the Illustration Finish panel (Scene Properties tab) to switch every outlined material to auto-harmonized line colors in a single click. This gives clothes, hair, and skin distinct rich tones instead of a uniform plain black line. DaskToon automatically syncs color branches to the companion outline materials.

Prepare Outline for Games

The Prepare Outline for Games operator (F3) writes smoothed normals into the DT_OutlineN UV map and line thickness into DT_OutlineW. When running Engine Export, DaskToon executes this step automatically for every mesh with outlines. Meshes require at least one UV map and no faces with more than 4 vertices.

Removing Outlines

To turn off outlines for a material, uncheck Outline on the Anime BSDF node or turn off Use Outline. Once all materials on a mesh have outlines disabled, the outline modifier is removed automatically. For legacy materials, use Remove DaskToon Outline from the material context menu.
