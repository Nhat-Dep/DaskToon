Clips

A clip is one action of a character: a walk, a wave. All the parts of the character play it together, and each clip becomes its own animation in the game.

The clip menu

In the Dope Sheet header, the clip menu shows the clip the character plays. Beside it are its name, the Loop button and +, which is New Clip: it makes a new, empty clip and plays it, and the animation you had so far becomes a clip first. In the menu, Use Clip plays a clip on the whole character, body and face, and Delete Clip deletes the one the character plays; it then plays the first clip left.

In the game

Engine Export writes each clip as an animation of its own: <Model>@<Clip>.fbx for Unity and an animation named after the clip in the glTF file for Godot. A clip with Loop on loops.
