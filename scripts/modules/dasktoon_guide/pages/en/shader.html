The Anime Shader

The Anime BSDF node gives a material flat anime shading in one node, with the lighting, rim, outline and color grading as switches.

The switches

On the Anime BSDF node, the six switches Ambient, Light, AO, Rim, Outline and Grade each turn one part on. A part that is off hides its inputs. Ambient tints the shadows with the World color, Light tints the lit side with the lamps' color, AO darkens creases, Rim adds a light edge, Outline adds the outline (see Outlines) and Grade grades the color (filter, shadow and highlight tint, saturation, brightness, contrast).

Ambient Mode and Light Mode choose how the World and the lamps color the surface: Overlay, hue only, multiply, add, or Pure Cel (Light Mode only), which keeps the cel shading without any color shift.

Simple and Ramp

Shading picks how light becomes shadow. Simple gives the inputs Shadow Color, Shadow Threshold and Shadow Softness. Ramp lets you paint the whole transition from shadow to light with a color ramp; a ramp with hard stops gives 2 or 3 flat tones.

With Ramp, the Style menu applies a ready-made style (Anime 2-Tone, Anime 3-Tone, Soft Painted, Anime Skin (Warm Edge), Manga) and your own styles. The first button next to it saves the ramp as one of your styles, shared by every file; the second turns the Simple sliders into a ramp.

Use DaskToon Shader

For a material that is not DaskToon's yet, open the material slot menu in Properties › Material and choose Use DaskToon Shader (or Use DaskToon Shader on All Selected). The material gets an Anime BSDF with an outline, fed its old color or texture. The old nodes stay in the material.

A material with a toon setup of its own (colors multiplied by the light) keeps its lit and shadow colors, read with the Sun and World of the current scene.

Combine Materials

Combine Materials in the same menu bakes all the material slots of a mesh into one atlas picture and one Anime BSDF material, so the viewport draws faster. Choose the atlas size (1024, 2048 or 4096) and the margin that hides seams. Restore Original Slots puts the earlier slots back.

The render engine

In Properties › Render, DaskToon Anime Engine is available beside the other engines; it renders with EEVEE and shows EEVEE's settings. New files start with the Standard view transform, so colors match the engine.
