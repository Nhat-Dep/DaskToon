Painting in Layers

Paint colors straight on the model in layers, with brushes made for anime. The layers live inside the picture, so Blender's own Texture Paint works with them.

Start Painting

Select the mesh and open the material slot menu in Properties › Material, then choose Start Painting (it is also in the Texture Slots panel while the mesh has no slots). DaskToon makes sure the mesh has a UV map, makes a picture of each anime material's color and connects it to the shader, adds the anime brushes and switches to Texture Paint with Anime Pen chosen.

Brushes

Anime Pen draws a hard, even line without a soft edge: outlines, eyes, flat shapes. Anime Blush is a faint soft spray that builds up: blush and soft shading. Anime Fill is Blender's fill brush. These brushes stay in the file.

Fill Part

The Fill Part tool fills the part of the model under the cursor with the brush color, on the active layer: click once on a skirt, a sleeve or a cheek. With the face mask on, it fills the selected faces.

Layers

The Paint Layers list is in the Texture Slots panel of the 3D view and in the Image panel of the Image Editor. Add Layer adds an empty layer above the active one (the first time, the picture becomes the layer Base). Each layer has an Opacity and a Blend mode, and the eye hides it; strokes still go to a hidden layer, but they do not show. Strokes always go to the active layer.

Duplicate copies the active layer, Merge Down merges it into the one below, and Flatten drops the layers and keeps what shows. Painted pictures are saved with the file. Layers work on single pictures, not on tiled ones.
