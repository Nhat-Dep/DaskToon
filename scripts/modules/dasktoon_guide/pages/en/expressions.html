8. Expressions

Expressions are shape keys: blink, joy, anger, surprise and the rest.

Eyes

DaskToon looks for loose eye pieces in front of the face (step 3), or a shape key of your own that closes the eyes.

Mouth

DaskToon looks for a mouth drawn on the face texture or built of pieces, or a shape key that opens the mouth. Joy smiles with it; for A I U E O, add a 2D mouth.

Expressions

Sculpt Eye Expressions makes blink, joy and the others from the eye pieces. Fill Empty Expressions fills the expression shape keys that move nothing (a model from elsewhere often has them), so every expression does something. Fix runs them.
