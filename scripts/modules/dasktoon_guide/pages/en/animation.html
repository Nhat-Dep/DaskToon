The Animation Tab

The Animation tab animates a whole character, body and face together, from one timeline.

The layout

The tab has the camera view on the left, the main 3D viewport, Properties on the right and one Dope Sheet as the timeline. It shows the armature, its children and the meshes with an Armature modifier. Files you open get this tab in place of Blender's own Animation tab.

Whole Character

With Whole Character on in the Dope Sheet header, the header also holds DaskToon's controls: Key Pose, the clips, the timing and the buttons for the onion skin and the fast action filter.

Key Pose

Key Pose keys the whole pose of the selected characters at the current frame: every bone, shape key and controller. Pressing I does the same in a scene without a keying set of its own. The bones of hair and skirt chains, which sway by themselves, and the hidden helpers that lift the shoulders are left out of the keys.
