Features

Everything of DaskToon's own, by kind of work.

Start

  • Projects and Models: Projects, models, drafts and saving.
  • The Interface: Where DaskToon's menus, tabs and panels are.

Shaders and Look

  • The Anime Shader: The Anime BSDF node, shading styles, Use DaskToon Shader, Combine Materials.
  • Other Anime Nodes: Materials, character features, single steps and manga nodes.
  • Outlines: The 3D outline, its color, colored lines and outline data for games.
  • Face Shading: Shade the face like an egg so the shadows curve smoothly.
  • Illustration Finish: Finish the whole picture like a painted illustration.

Rig

  • The Anime Rig: The skeleton, parts, joints and Build Rig.
  • Body Regions: Recognize and paint head, torso, arms and legs.
  • The Rig Check: Test poses and the spots to fix.
  • Hair and Skirt Sway: Springs, gravity, colliders and baking.

Expressions

  • Eyes, Mouth and Gaze: Look target, flat eyes, 2D mouth and lip sync.
  • Expression Tools: VRM and ARKit sets, fill, sculpt, split and test.
  • Expression Controllers: Drive shape keys with a handle or sliders.

Painting and UV

  • Painting in Layers: Paint layers inside the picture, with anime brushes.
  • Anime Unwrap: The UV layout made for anime and game characters.

Animation

  • The Animation Tab: Whole Character, Key Pose and the timeline.
  • Clips: One action of a character, one animation in the game.
  • Anime Timing and Onion Skin: Twos, threes, holds and ghosts of the keyed poses.
  • Fast Action and Effects: Smear, afterimages, speed lines and anime effects.

Export

  • Export to Unity: Engine Export for Unity 6.
  • Export to Godot: Engine Export for Godot 4.
