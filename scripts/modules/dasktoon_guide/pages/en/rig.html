7. Rigging

The rig is the skeleton that moves the character. DaskToon builds it for you.

Rig

Select the character (or nothing, for every visible mesh) and use Add › Armature › Anime Humanoid. It finds the body, builds the skeleton, paints the weights, makes the bend fixes and runs the Check, in one go. Fix runs it.

Rig Check

The rig's Check (Anime Rig › Check) bends the character in test poses and lists the spots that tear or dent. Look at each and paint the weights there. Fix runs the Check again.

Edge loops at the knees and elbows

Each knee and elbow needs 2 or 3 edge loops, so it bends round and the bend fixes have vertices to move. Add them with Loop Cut (Ctrl R) and rig again. No Fix: it is modelling.
