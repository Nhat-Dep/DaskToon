Body Regions

The rig needs to know which part of a mesh is the head, the torso, an arm or a leg. DaskToon recognizes the regions itself; you can paint them where it is wrong.

Recognize

In the Regions panel, Recognize paints the head, torso, arms and legs on the Body and Clothing meshes, and turns the pieces that hang from the head (hair locks, ears) into locks of their own. Show Regions shows the regions in the 3D views, in Solid shading colored by region. Press Build Rig afterwards: the weights follow the regions.

Paint Regions

Under Paint Regions there is a button for each region: Head, Torso, Left Arm, Right Arm, Left Leg and Right Leg. Press one to go into Vertex Paint with that region's color. Select faces and press Shift K to fill them with it. Done Painting leaves Vertex Paint and selects the rig again.

Locks

In Edit Mode, Lock from Selection (in the Anime Rig panel of the mesh's Object Data) makes the pieces under the selected vertices one lock that is rigid on the head; give it the role Hair and it sways. Add Part from Selection makes the selected vertices a part of the rig, kept in a new vertex group.
