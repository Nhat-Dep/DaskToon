Eyes, Mouth and Gaze

Eyes that look at a target, flat eyes drawn on the face, a mouth drawn in cells, and a voice that moves the mouth.

Gaze

Build Rig makes a LookTarget bone in front of the face and makes the two eyes track it. Move the bone and the eyes follow, within the limits of the Gaze panel: Side Limit and Up and Down Limit. You can also turn the eye bones yourself; the exported animation bakes the turned eyes, so the game needs no constraint.

Flat Eye

An iris drawn on the face (an eye plate in front of the skin) gets the role Flat Eye. It slides on its UVs as the eye turns. Side says which eye it is (the side the part lies on, by default) and Range how far the iris slides when the eye turns all the way; a negative range turns the direction round.

2D Mouth

A mouth drawn on a plate gets the role 2D Mouth. Its picture is an atlas of cells; set Rows and Columns to match. The cells, from the top left, are closed, A, I, U, E and O. The object's dt_mouth property picks the cell. The Jaw role is for a lower jaw and chin that follow the Jaw bone.

Lip Sync from Audio

Lip Sync from Audio (in Expression Tools, under the shape keys of the mesh) listens to a voice file and keys the mouth: the vowel shape keys, the jaw and the 2D mouth, in the clip being played. Choose the Voice File (empty takes the first sound strip of the Sequencer), the Start Frame of the voice and the Strength: 1 is the full vowel shapes.
