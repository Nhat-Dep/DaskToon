Anime Timing and Onion Skin

Anime is drawn on twos and threes, with holds. DaskToon keeps that in the clip, in the viewport and in the game.

Timing

The Timing button of the header (its text is the step at the current frame: 1s, 2s, Hold…) opens a panel. Set Timing Here with 1s, 2s, 3s, 4s or Hold makes the clip run on that step from the current frame, until the next timing row; Remove Timing removes a row and the timing before it goes on. Key on the drawn frames: a key between them shows from the next drawn frame.

In the game

The Engine Export keeps the held frames: they stay constant in Unity, and Godot holds the pose from one frame to the next.

Onion Skin

Onion Skin shows faintly in the 3D viewports the character's poses at the keys before and after this frame. Its panel has Before and After (how many keyed poses to show, up to 8 each) and Opacity of the nearest ghost; farther ones fade. The ghosts update when you stop for a moment.
