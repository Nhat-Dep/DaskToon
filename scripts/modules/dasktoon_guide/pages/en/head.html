3. Head and Face

The face is what makes an anime character. DaskToon animates it for you when it can find the eyes and the mouth.

The eyes

Build each eye of loose, flat pieces just in front of the face: the white, the iris, the upper and the lower lashes. Sculpt Eye Expressions then closes them for blink and curves them for joy. You can also draw the eyes on the face texture and sculpt the closed eyes yourself. The check comes after the rig (step 8): DaskToon finds the eyes in the head's frame, which the rig gives.

The mouth

Draw the mouth on the face texture, or build it of pieces in front of the face: DaskToon makes joy smile with either. For speech (A I U E O), add a 2D mouth, a picture that changes.

Mirror applied

Modelling one half with a Mirror modifier is fine, but apply it before rigging (the modifier's menu › Apply). The bend fixes are shape keys, and DaskToon skips a mesh with a Mirror still on. Fix applies it on meshes without shape keys.
