Fast Action and Effects

Where a character moves fast, anime smears the picture, leaves afterimages and draws speed lines. The Fast Action filter does this for you; effects such as a shockwave or falling petals are added from a menu.

The Fast Action filter

In the Dope Sheet header, the Fast Action button turns the filter on for the character; the button beside it opens its panel. Where a part of the character is faster than Threshold, it is smeared (Smear: how far the trailing side is pulled toward the frame before), shows Afterimages of the frames before (Afterimage Opacity and Afterimage Color) and trails speed lines (Line Density, Line Length, Line Color). The threshold scales with the height of the character.

Baking

The effects show in DaskToon's viewport and in its render; they do not go into the exported file. Bake for Render works them out for every frame of the scene and keeps them; Render bakes first where the bake is missing or old. Bake Again redoes the bake and Free Bake drops it.

Anime effects

Add › Anime Effect in the 3D viewport adds an effect: Shockwave Ring, Hit Spark, Slash Arc and Debris Blast for impacts, and Bubbles Rising, Bubbles Sinking, Sakura Petals, Magic Sparkles, Falling Leaves, Cel Rain and Fire Embers for the air. They are put in a collection of their own.
