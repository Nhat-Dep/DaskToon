Illustration Finish

The finish treats the whole rendered picture like a painter finishing an illustration: tinted shadows and lights, air, soft glow, strokes and paper. It lives in the scene's compositor and every setting is yours to change.

Where it is

In Properties › Scene, the Illustration Finish panel. Its checkbox in the header turns the finish on. Style picks a style (Default, Early Morning, Sunset, Night, Watercolor, and your own); the + button next to it saves the current settings as a style of your own, shared by every file. Overall Strength says how much of the finish shows: 0 is the picture as rendered.

Show in Viewport chooses which 3D viewports in Rendered shading show the finish.

The layers

Four groups hold twelve layers, each with its own switch and settings. Painting Colors: Shadow and Light Tint, Light Gradient, Saturation and Contrast. Painting Depth: Air, Soft Background, Vignette. Painting Light: Soft Glow, Light Rays, Back Light Wrap. Strokes and Texture: Brush Strokes, Chromatic Aberration, Paper.

Light Gradient follows the Sun when Follow Sun is on, otherwise it uses the angle you set. Soft Background follows the camera's focus when Auto Focus is on, otherwise the focus distance you set. Air and Soft Background read the depth of the picture.

The scene's compositor

The finish builds its own node group in the scene's compositor. If the scene already has a compositor of its own, a box appears with Add to Compositor: it puts the finish into your nodes.

Colored lines

Colored Lines for All Materials gives every outlined material a line in a deep shade of its own colors, instead of one fixed color.
